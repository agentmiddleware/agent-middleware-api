## Stop rules and final decision

Stop before execution if any entry row is not `PASS`; the source/configuration
is not bound to accepted technical evidence; the ingress limit has not been
negatively tested; the data or deployment is unsupported; the baseline is not
the partner's strongest acceptable solution; effect truth/finality is vague;
or the fault cannot be armed and reset safely.

Stop during execution on a second gateway dispatch/debit for one accepted key,
unauthorized or out-of-scope execution, false refund/consumption state,
unverifiable or inconsistent evidence, an unbounded or sensitive payload,
unsafe staging state, inability to restore the fault control, missing
authoritative observations, or a material A/B protocol divergence. Preserve
the failure; do not weaken assertions, alter the baseline, or silently rerun it
as a pass.

Stop product expansion after the comparison if Arm A already clears the block;
the only difference is receipt format; Arm B's latency, reconciliation,
security, availability, or operating burden exceeds the precommitted limit; the
partner will not retain the boundary; or no buyer, approved amount, and dated
payment/commitment exist. A technical pass alone does not satisfy the company
decision.
